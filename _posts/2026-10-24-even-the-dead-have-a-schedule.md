This is quite strong for a first blog post, but since it happened, it's been at the back of my mind to write about. 
This happened on a Saturday and as is custom on a Saturday, funerals happen and the dead have to be buried. It's not odd to see a line of cars following a flashy, decorated, extended SUV. What's happening more now from what I have seen is having a police escort. Cemeteries have a closing time and if the dead are not buried, they'll have to go back to the funeral home. This leads to what I saw.
I was on my way to pick up my mom from church when I encountered a particular procession at a stoplight. I'd already passed two of them on my way, when I hit the traffic light on my turn. There were two officers on motorcycles leading them. One, the visibly older of the two, stopped, while the other made a hand signal and continued to lead the line of vehicles past me. The older officer continued to wave them through as the lights changed, and i marveled at the amount of vehicles that passed. One could say that perhaps they have a lot of people who owned cars that could attend, but seeing the amount has me feeling a particular way. The dead have a schedule, and everyone was working together for this one thing for this last time. 
Death is a somber topic and one that I can't really speak freely about with others as it either scares them or me depending on the situation. But seeing a procession pass me by allows it to take the forefront for a short while, without it being jarring or triggering other discussions on important societal issues. The procession gives me a glimpse of people working together, silently and efficiently. No one has to argue or be demanding, there is no infighting in the moment. Just for this person, one last time.
